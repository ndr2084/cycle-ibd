Our

Mission

The importance of outreach, education, and new treatments cannot be understated. Our mission is to fuel Crohn’s and Colitis Canada with donations so they continue to improve the lives of all Canadians living with IBD.

Who

We Are

Created by Nathan Rous, he is a member of the Volunteer Organization Committee at Crohn’s and Colitis Canada. His role involves helping improve connections between his region and the organization.

Our Plan

With a passion for biking and a background in graphic design and software engineering, Nathan took it upon myself to create IBD Cycle - a web platform for users to compete against each other to see who can raise the most money and bike the most miles.
